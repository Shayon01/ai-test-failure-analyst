01 — About

Business understanding, technical curiosity, product execution.

I'm a Business Analyst working on digital payment products, where my work sits between the people who need a product and the teams who build it. I spend my days understanding customer and merchant problems, translating business needs into clear requirements, and working closely with engineering teams to bring features from concept to implementation.

My path into product started with a foundation in Computer Science and grew through an MBA in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science. Today I'm focused on Product Management and AI-enabled product development — exploring how data and generative AI can make products more useful, not just more complex.

I care about structured thinking, clear documentation, and asking the right questions early — so that what gets built genuinely solves the problem it was meant to.
